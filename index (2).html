20-Minute Health & Longevity Program

Built for daily walking, Bowflex dumbbells up to 52.5 lb, no bench required, and the goal order: health → longevity → fat loss → muscle.

Changing rounds clears the checklist for the new round.
General Rules
  • Workout = exactly 20 minutes.
  • Use good form.
  • Minimal rest unless specified.
  • Increase reps before increasing weight.
  • Walk daily outside of workouts.
Progression Targets
  • Push-Ups: 23 → 30 → 40+
  • Suitcase Carry with 52.5 lb: 45 sec → 60 sec → 90 sec
  • Farmer Carry with both 52.5 lb dumbbells: 60 sec → 90 sec
  • Split Squats & Lunges: 8 reps → 10 reps → 12 reps, then increase weight
  • RDL: 10 reps → 12 reps → 15 reps, then increase weight
Every 2 Weeks
  • Weight: current 231 lb
  • Waist: current 44"
  • Push-Ups: current 23
  • Plank: current 3:00
  • Mile: current 7:50
  • Suitcase Carry with 52.5 lb: record baseline
Primary Goal
  • Waist: 44" → under 40"
  • Weight: 231 lb → under 210 lb
  • Maintain strength and conditioning while reducing waist and body weight.
